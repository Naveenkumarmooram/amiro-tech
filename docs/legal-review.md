# Required review before adopting website policies

The three legal pages are draft website notices, not a legal compliance certification. They are visibly marked pending company review. Do not remove that status or publish as final until the owner and a qualified legal reviewer approve them.

Confirm:
- The privacy/grievance contact, responsible person, and applicable response process.
- Actual retention/deletion periods for enquiries, resumes, inbox backups and provider logs.
- Hosting/email providers, deployment analytics/security cookies, storage locations and cross-border arrangements.
- Actual direct-form activation, access controls, and security practices.
- Whether application data is retained for future roles and how permission is handled.
- Applicable Indian requirements and their commencement dates, plus laws relevant to customers elsewhere.
- Commercial cancellation/refund terms in signed service agreements; do not invent blanket no-refund terms for this brochure site.

Sources checked: https://www.meity.gov.in/documents/act-and-policies/digital-personal-data-protection-rules-2025-gDOxUjMtQWa and https://www.pib.gov.in/newsite/erelcontent.aspx?lang=2&reg=48&relid=74990 .

No optional tracking code or browser storage writes were found in the application source during this review. Hosting-level behaviour has not been audited. No consent banner was added merely for appearance; introduce appropriate controls before optional tracking where required.
