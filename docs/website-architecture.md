## 2. Homepage Architecture

The homepage should function as a business narrative, not a generic marketing landing page. It should answer three questions quickly:

- Who is Amiro Tech?
- What kinds of problems does it solve?
- Why should a business trust it?

### Recommended Section Order

#### 1. Hero Section
- Why it exists: Establish positioning immediately and create relevance.
- Problem it solves: Helps visitors quickly understand that Amiro Tech delivers software and AI solutions for real business problems.
- Expected user emotion: Confidence, clarity, interest.
- Information hierarchy: Company name, positioning statement, proof point, primary CTA, secondary CTA.
- CTA: Book a strategy call.

#### 2. Value Proposition Strip
- Why it exists: Reinforce the company’s core promise in one glance.
- Problem it solves: Prevents ambiguity around the service scope.
- Expected user emotion: Reassurance and trust.
- Information hierarchy: Short statements such as custom software, AI solutions, process automation, integration, digital transformation.
- CTA: Explore capabilities.

#### 3. “Why Amiro Tech” / Strategic Positioning
- Why it exists: Differentiate the company from generic agencies or software shops.
- Problem it solves: Explains that the company is not only building tools, but building intelligent software around business processes.
- Expected user emotion: Respect, confidence, curiosity.
- Information hierarchy: Positioning statement, differentiation points, business-first framing.
- CTA: Learn about our approach.

#### 4. Services Overview
- Why it exists: Show the scope of capabilities without overwhelming the visitor.
- Problem it solves: Helps visitors map their needs to the company’s offerings.
- Expected user emotion: Recognition and clarity.
- Information hierarchy: Core service categories, short descriptions, links to deeper service pages.
- CTA: Explore services.

#### 5. How We Work / Delivery Approach
- Why it exists: Reduce perceived delivery risk.
- Problem it solves: Gives prospective clients a clear idea of how collaboration works.
- Expected user emotion: Safety, confidence, ease.
- Information hierarchy: Discovery, design, delivery, iteration, partnership.
- CTA: See our process.

#### 6. Proof / Selected Work / Business Outcomes
- Why it exists: Build trust through evidence.
- Problem it solves: Replaces generic claims with proof of delivery and impact.
- Expected user emotion: Validation and confidence.
- Information hierarchy: Outcome-first case study summaries, problem, solution, impact.
- CTA: View case studies.

#### 7. Technology & Capability Pillars
- Why it exists: Reinforce the company’s modern technical depth.
- Problem it solves: Helps technical buyers understand that the company can handle complex systems and modern AI workflows.
- Expected user emotion: Competence, assurance.
- Information hierarchy: Platform engineering, AI systems, integration, analytics, cloud delivery.
- CTA: Explore technical capabilities.

#### 8. Final Conversion Section
- Why it exists: Convert the visitor from interest to action.
- Problem it solves: Removes the final barrier to contact.
- Expected user emotion: Momentum and confidence.
- Information hierarchy: Clear invitation, concise value framing, direct CTA, trust signals.
- CTA: Book a consultation.

## 3. Services Page Architecture

The Services page should feel like a business consulting experience, not a catalog of features.

### Core Content Principle

Every service should answer the same progression:

Business problem

↓

Solution

↓

Business outcome

↓

Call to action

### Recommended Section Order

1. Introduction / service framing
2. Service groups
3. Detailed service cards
4. Cross-sell / adjacent capabilities
5. Conversion block

### Service Grouping

#### Group A — Product & Platform Engineering
- Custom Software Development
- Enterprise Web Applications
- SaaS Development

Purpose: Help companies build products, platforms, and digital products that scale.

#### Group B — Intelligence & Automation
- AI & Generative AI Solutions
- Voice AI Solutions
- AI Agents & Agentic Workflows
- Business Process Automation

Purpose: Help companies use AI and automation to increase speed, consistency, and decision quality.

#### Group C — Integration & Insight
- API & System Integrations
- Dashboards & Reporting
- Digital Transformation

Purpose: Help companies connect systems, unlock visibility, and improve operating performance.

### Service Card Structure

Each service should include:

- Business problem: What challenge the client is facing
- Solution: What Amiro Tech delivers
- Business outcome: How the client benefits
- CTA: What the visitor should do next

### Example Service Pattern

#### Custom Software Development
- Business problem: Off-the-shelf software does not fit complex operations.
- Solution: Tailored software aligned to internal workflows and business logic.
- Business outcome: Better efficiency, stronger control, and improved scalability.
- CTA: Discuss a custom solution

#### AI & Generative AI Solutions
- Business problem: Teams need practical AI adoption without experimentation risk.
- Solution: Business-focused AI implementations that fit real workflows.
- Business outcome: Faster execution, better knowledge access, stronger productivity.
- CTA: Explore AI opportunities

#### Business Process Automation
- Business problem: Teams lose time to repetitive, manual processes.
- Solution: Workflow automation that reduces friction and human error.
- Business outcome: Lower operational cost and higher consistency.
- CTA: Automate a workflow

- A team that combines product thinking, software engineering, and AI implementation

#### Why We Exist
- To help businesses modernize operations with intelligent software that powers real processes
- To bridge the gap between technical execution and business outcomes

- The work starts with operational need, not technology trends
- Every solution is shaped by process, adoption, and measurable value

#### How We Work
- Discovery-led engagement
- Practical architecture and delivery planning
- Collaborative execution with clear ownership and transparency

#### Long-Term Partnership
- The relationship should feel ongoing and strategic
- The company should be positioned as a partner for evolution, not a one-off vendor

2. Why Amiro Tech exists
3. Approach to building intelligent software
4. Delivery philosophy and operating model

7. Contact CTA

### Trust-Building Content Themes

- Business-minded problem framing
- Clear delivery discipline
- Thoughtful implementation rather than feature dumping
- A realistic, mature approach to AI and transformation

## 5. Contact Page Architecture

The contact experience should feel easy, professional, and business-focused.

### Information Hierarchy

1. Primary reason to contact
2. Preferred contact methods
3. What happens after submission
4. Project-type selection
5. Short form to capture details

### Contact Methods

- Email address
- Booking link for discovery call
- Optional contact form
- LinkedIn or professional social presence if relevant

### Inquiry Form

The form should be lightweight and intentional. It should capture:

- Name
- Company
- Work email
- Project type
- Brief business challenge
- Preferred timeline
- Preferred contact method

### Requirement Categories

Suggested categories:

- Custom software development
- Enterprise web application
- SaaS product development
- AI solution / generative AI
- Voice AI
- Automation workflow
- System integration
- Dashboards and reporting
- Digital transformation
- Other

### Expected Conversion Flow

1. User arrives with a business problem
2. They select a relevant service category
3. They share context briefly
4. They submit and receive a clear follow-up expectation
5. The next step is a discovery conversation or proposal request

### Conversion Principle

The page should feel like a structured intake experience, not a generic contact form.

## 6. Global Navigation

The navigation should be simple, calm, and enterprise-friendly.

### Desktop Navigation

Primary navigation:

- Home
- Services
- About
- Process
- Case Studies
- Contact

Optional high-priority CTA:

- Book a Consultation

### Mobile Navigation

The mobile menu should:

- Collapse into a simple drawer or panel
- Keep primary pages visible
- Preserve the primary CTA in a prominent position
- Avoid clutter and secondary links in the first layer

### Footer Navigation

Footer should include:

- Company overview
- Services
- About / Approach
- Case Studies
- Contact
- Legal links

### Future Scalability

As the website grows, navigation should support additional sections without becoming noisy:

- Industries
- Insights / Blog
- Careers
- Partner Program

These should be added only when the content exists and the navigation demand is justified.

## 7. Website Content Architecture

Content should be structured and data-driven. No hardcoded text should live inside components.

### Recommended Data Organization

#### Navigation
- File: src/data/navigation.ts
- Purpose: Holds all nav items, ordering, labels, and CTA links

#### Hero
- File: src/data/hero.ts
- Purpose: Stores the hero headline, subheadline, CTAs, and supporting trust points

#### Services
- File: src/data/services.ts
- Purpose: Stores all service categories, descriptions, outcomes, and links

#### Process
- File: src/data/process.ts
- Purpose: Stores the delivery process, phases, and explanatory copy

#### Technology
- File: src/data/technology.ts
- Purpose: Stores product engineering, AI, integration, and platform capabilities

#### Workflow
- File: src/data/workflow.ts
- Purpose: Stores the engagement model, collaboration steps, and client journey copy

#### Case Study
- File: src/data/caseStudies.ts
- Purpose: Stores proof points, client scenarios, outcomes, and metadata

#### Footer
- File: src/data/footer.ts
- Purpose: Stores footer navigation, company info, and legal links

### Content Model Principles

- Keep content separated from presentation
- Use structured objects or arrays rather than ad hoc strings in components
- Make copy easy to update by marketing or product stakeholders
- Support future localization if needed

## 8. UX Principles

These principles should guide the entire website experience.

### Layout Principles

- Maximum section width: 1200px to 1280px for main content areas
- Maximum text width: 70 characters per line for body copy
- Heading lengths: Keep headings concise and outcome-focused
- Paragraph lengths: Prefer short, scannable paragraphs
- Spacing system: Use a consistent scale for vertical rhythm and section separation

### Interaction Principles

- Button consistency: Limit primary actions to a small set and keep them visually consistent
- CTA placement: Place primary CTAs where intent is highest, especially after value framing and proof
- Visual hierarchy: Use clear content order with strong section progression
- Accessibility: Ensure color contrast, keyboard navigation, semantic structure, and readable typography

### Responsive Philosophy

- Desktop-first foundation with strong mobile refinement
- Content should remain structured and scannable on smaller screens
- Navigation should prioritize clarity over density on mobile

### Animation Philosophy

- Motion should be subtle, purposeful, and non-distracting
- Use transitions for clarity and feedback, not decoration
- Respect reduced-motion preferences

### Tone and Messaging Principles

- Professional, confident, and calm
- Business-oriented rather than hype-driven
- Clear about outcomes, not only features
- Avoid vague tech language unless explained in business terms

## 9. Final Architecture Review

### Possible UX Problems to Watch

- Too much technical jargon too early
- Generic claims without proof
- Overloading the homepage with every service at once
- Missing a strong conversion path after trust-building content
- Too many CTAs competing for attention

### Content Gaps to Address

- Clear examples of business impact
- A stronger explanation of delivery process
- More direct proof of complex solution capability
- Stronger differentiation from typical agencies and software contractors

### Missing Trust Elements

- Client outcomes and case studies
- Delivery process visibility
- Team credibility and expertise framing
- Clear explanation of how engagements begin

### Future Expansion Opportunities

- Industry-specific landing pages
- Detailed service pages by domain or use case
- Thought leadership and insights content
- Case study depth and testimonial expansion
- Partner or ecosystem content

### Scalability Considerations

- Keep content models reusable for new pages
- Use structured content rather than hardcoded page-specific copy
- Keep the navigation lean and add sections only when supported by content
- Design the architecture to support future expansion without reworking the foundation

## Recommended Direction

The architecture should present Amiro Tech as a premium, strategic technology partner that builds intelligent software around business processes. The experience should feel:

- Enterprise-ready
- Business-focused
- Calm and confident
- Trusted and strategic
- Clear in how value is created

This foundation will support a website that is modern, credible, and scalable from day one.
